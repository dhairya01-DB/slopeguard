Landslide Preparedness & Safety Guidelines

Plain-language emergency protocols aligned with NDMA disaster management SOPs.

1. Before the Rain

  • Inspect drainage ditches around your home for blockages from mud and leaves.
  • Watch for newly cracked walls, leaning utility poles, or doors sticking in frames.
  • Assemble an emergency grab bag: torch, battery radio, water, vital documents, medicines.

2. During a Slide

  • Listen for unusual sounds: cracking trees, roaring mud, or sudden changes in stream flow.
  • Move away quickly from the path of debris. Move to higher, solid ground immediately.
  • Never try to drive across a road inundated with sliding mud or rushing brown water.

3. After the Slide

  • Stay away from the slide area. Secondary slides can occur hours later without warning.
  • Check for injured or trapped persons without entering directly into unstable slope mud.
  • Report broken power cables or gas leaks immediately to emergency services.
